# Seattle Asian Pages (東 · 98074)

A curated, responsive regional directory and Google Maps review index for **Asian daily life, dining, shopping, family care, and home services within 50 miles of Sammamish, WA (ZIP 98074)** — spanning **Chinese, Taiwanese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Thai** communities across Puget Sound.

**Live Site:** [https://coastwalker.github.io/seattlepagesforasian/](https://coastwalker.github.io/seattlepagesforasian/)

## Coverage & Categories

Anchored at **ZIP 98074 (Sammamish Plateau)** and covering a verified **50-mile radius** across Sammamish, Issaquah, Redmond, Bellevue, Kirkland, Seattle (Chinatown-ID, Little Saigon, U-District, Capitol Hill, Ballard, Fremont, Rainier Valley), Renton, Kent, Tukwila, Bothell, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Federal Way, Lakewood, and Tacoma (Lincoln International District):

1. **Restaurants (`餐廳美食`)** — Chinese (Sichuan, Cantonese & Dim Sum, Taiwanese, Shanghainese, Hunan, Hot Pot, Hand-Pulled Noodles), Japanese (Edomae Sushi & Omakase, Ramen & Tsukemen, Izakaya, Tendon & Katsu), Korean (Korean BBQ, Soondubu, Kalbijjim, Contemporary Korean), **Vietnamese** (Phở, Bánh Mì, Modern Vietnamese, Vermicelli & Street Food), and **Thai** (Northern Thai, Classic Thai, Thai Night-Market Bistros).
2. **Stores & Markets (`超市百貨`)** — Supermarkets & grocers (T&T, Asian Family Market, Uwajimaya, H Mart, 99 Ranch, Lam's Seafood Market, Viet-Wah, Hau Hau Market, Hong Kong Market, Pal-Do World, M2M) plus bookstores, stationery, ceramics, and lifestyle shops (Kinokuniya, Daiso, Kobo at Higo, Great Wall Shopping Mall).
3. **Bakeries, Coffee & Tea (`烘焙咖啡茶飲`)** — Chinese, Taiwanese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Thai bakeries, patisseries, bánh mì bakehouses, specialty Vietnamese phin/egg coffee roasteries, *chè* & bingsu dessert cafes, and artisanal tea houses.
4. **Barbers & Salons (`理髮美髮`)** — Asian hair salons, Korean digital/down perm specialists, Japanese precision stylists, and Vietnamese/Chinese/Korean straight-hair barbershops.
5. **Nanny & Home Care (`月嫂家政`)** — Postpartum confinement nanny (*Yuesao* 月嫂 / *Zuo Yue Zi* 坐月子) & Korean *Sanhujori* specialists, confinement meal services, bilingual nanny agencies, Mandarin immersion childcare, and bilingual Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese & Thai family/senior care organizations.
6. **Remodel & Home (`裝修建材`)** — Bilingual general contractors, Japanese/Korean/Chinese/Vietnamese kitchen, bath & hardwood flooring specialists, high-CFM Asian wok range hood showrooms (Pacific & FOTILE), and solid-wood cabinetry & stone warehouses.
7. **Wellness & Spas (`泰式按摩與中醫`)** — Authentic Traditional Thai Massage (*Nuad Boran*) studios, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) & acupuncture clinics with herbal dispensaries, Korean *jjimjilbang* bathhouses, and Japanese/Korean head spas.

## Key Features

- **Google Maps Review Integration** — Displays average Google Maps star ratings, total review counts, reviewer highlight tags, consensus review summaries, and one-tap links to live Google Maps listings and directions from `98074`.
- **Responsive Across Phone, Tablet & Desktop** — Mobile-first bottom-sheet drawers & filter sheets, adaptive tablet grids, and desktop split-view / index table modes.
- **Light & Dark Themes** — Zero-flash automatic system theme detection (`prefers-color-scheme`) with persistent manual toggle (*Washi & Cedar* light theme / *Sumi & Lantern* dark theme).
- **Bilingual Interface (`EN` / `中文`)** — Switch UI labels seamlessly while searching across English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, or Thai keywords.
- **Interactive 50-Mile Map** — Leaflet map mode with theme-synced light/dark tiles and a visual 50-mile boundary around `98074`.
